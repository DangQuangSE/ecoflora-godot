## Goi y cap nhat

Khi game thay doi gameplay, hay cap nhat cac file player guide truoc khi release:

- Them hoa/vat pham moi: cap nhat guide vuon va tui do.
- Doi gia shop/loai vat pham: cap nhat guide cua hang.
- Doi co che task/focus: cap nhat guide nhiem vu va Focus Mode.
- Them man choi moi: tao guide rieng va them vao muc luc nay.
