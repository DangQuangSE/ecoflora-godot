# Huong dan nhiem vu va Focus Mode

Nhiem vu giup ban co muc tieu hang ngay. Focus Mode giup bien thoi gian hoc tap thanh phan thuong trong game.

## Nhiem vu ngay va tuan

Mo bang nhiem vu bang nut task tren HUD.

Trong bang nhiem vu co:

- Nhiem vu ngay: thuong reset theo ngay.
- Nhiem vu tuan: muc tieu dai hon, thuong cao hon.
- Thanh tien do: cho biet ban da lam du bao nhieu.
- Nut nhan thuong: sang khi nhiem vu hoan thanh.

## Cach hoan thanh nhiem vu

Nhiem vu co the yeu cau:

- Cham soc cay.
- Trong hoa.
- Thu hoach.
- Su dung vat pham.
- Dat tien do trong game.
- Hoan thanh Focus Mode neu tinh nang duoc bat.

Sau khi hoan thanh:

1. Mo bang nhiem vu.
2. Tim nhiem vu co the nhan thuong.
3. Bam nhan.
4. Kiem tra coin, XP hoac vat pham moi trong tui do.

## Focus Mode

Focus Mode la che do tap trung hoc tap.

Luong choi co ban:

1. Di den khu truong hoc.
2. Vao lop hoc.
3. Chon thoi luong tap trung.
4. Bat dau phien Focus.
5. Khong thoat game/mo app khac trong luc Focus neu game dang theo doi phien.
6. Hoan thanh du thoi gian de nhan thuong.

## Phan thuong Focus

Phan thuong co the gom:

- Binh tuoi.
- Phan bon.
- Thuoc.
- Decor.
- XP hoac coin tuy cau hinh.

Thoi gian Focus cang dai thi phan thuong co the cang tot, tuy cau hinh tu admin.

## Khi Focus that bai

Neu thoat app, chuyen app qua nhieu lan, hoac vi pham dieu kien tap trung, phien Focus co the that bai.

Khi that bai, game co the:

- Khong trao thuong.
- Tru XP cua cay trong vuon.
- Hien thong bao khi ban quay lai vuon.

Meo: truoc khi bat dau Focus, hay sac pin, tat cac app gay xao nhang va chon thoi luong phu hop.
