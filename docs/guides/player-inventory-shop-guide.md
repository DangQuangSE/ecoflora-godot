# Huong dan tui do va cua hang

Tui do va cua hang la noi quan ly tai nguyen cua player.

## Tui do

Tui do gom cac nhom vat pham:

| Nhom | Vi du | Cach dung |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hat giong | Huong duong, hoa hong, tulip | Chon hat roi cham vao o dat trong |
| Vat pham cham soc | Binh tuoi, phan bon, thuoc | Chon vat pham roi cham/keo qua cay |
| Decor | Hang rao, da, thap nuoc | Chon decor roi cham vi tri muon dat |
| San pham thu hoach | Bong hoa da thu hoach | Luu trong inventory de dung cho tinh nang lien quan |

## Loc vat pham

Trong tui do co cac tab de xem nhanh:

- Tat ca: hien moi vat pham dang co.
- Hat giong: chi hien hat giong.
- Vat pham: chi hien vat pham cham soc/tieu hao.
- Decor: chi hien do trang tri.

Neu tui do trong, hay vao shop mua them hoac hoan thanh nhiem vu de nhan thuong.

## Chon va huy chon vat pham

Khi chon mot vat pham, HUD se hien o vat pham dang chon.

- Cham vao o dat/cay de su dung vat pham dang chon.
- Dung nut huy chon neu muon bo vat pham khoi tay.
- Khi mo panel khac, game co the an tam thoi o vat pham dang chon de tranh thao tac nham.

## Cua hang

Cua hang dung coin de mua:

- Hat giong moi.
- Vat pham cham soc.
- Decor.
- Cac goi/vat pham dac biet neu duoc cau hinh.

Cach mua:

1. Cham nut shop tren HUD.
2. Chon tab vat pham muon mua.
3. Kiem tra gia va so coin hien co.
4. Bam mua.
5. Vat pham se duoc them vao tui do neu giao dich thanh cong.

## Coin

Coin hien tren HUD.

Ban co the nhan coin tu:

- Nhiem vu ngay/tuan.
- Phan thuong he thong.
- Cac su kien hoac gift code neu co.

Meo: neu moi choi, nen uu tien mua hat giong va vat pham cham soc truoc decor de tang tien do nhanh hon.
