# FAQ cho nguoi choi

## Toi moi vao game, nen lam gi truoc?

Hay vao vuon, mo tui do, trong hat giong dau tien vao o dat trong, sau do dung vat pham cham soc de cay tang XP.

## Lam sao de mo them khu dat?

Tich luy XP bang cach trong va cham soc hoa. Khi dat yeu cau, khu moi se duoc mo va lop may/khoa se bien mat.

## Vi sao toi khong trong duoc cay?

Co the do:

- O dat dang bi khoa.
- O dat da co cay.
- Ban chua chon hat giong.
- Hat giong trong tui do da het.
- Game dang dong bo du lieu va chua cho thao tac.

## Vi sao dung vat pham cham soc khong co tac dung?

Kiem tra cac diem sau:

- Ban da chon dung vat pham trong tui do chua.
- Cay/o dat co hop le khong.
- So luong vat pham con lon hon 0 khong.
- Hanh dong co dang bi cooldown khong, neu server dang bat co che cooldown.

## Thu hoach nhu the nao?

Chi cay da truong thanh moi thu hoach duoc. Bat nut luoi liem, cham vao cay da du dieu kien, sau do kiem tra tui do.

## Nut do tren nhiem vu la gi?

Do la dau hieu co nhiem vu da hoan thanh va co the nhan thuong.

## Lam sao mua them hat giong hoac vat pham?

Mo shop tren HUD, chon mon muon mua va bam mua neu du coin.

## Coin dung de lam gi?

Coin dung de mua hat giong, vat pham cham soc, decor va cac vat pham khac trong shop.

## Lam sao trang tri vuon?

Mua hoac nhan decor, mo tui do, chon decor, sau do cham vao vi tri trong vuon de dat.

## Vi sao Focus Mode co the tru XP?

Focus Mode khuyen khich ban tap trung. Neu phien Focus that bai do vi pham dieu kien, game co the ap dung hinh phat nhu tru XP cay, tuy cau hinh.

## Toi co the xem lai huong dan trong game khong?

Co. Hay mo Tips/huong dan tu HUD neu nut nay dang kha dung. Lan dau vao vuon, game cung co thong bao moi ban doc huong dan.
