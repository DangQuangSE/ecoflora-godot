# Huong dan vuon hoa cho nguoi choi

Vuon la noi ban trong hoa, cham soc cay, thu hoach va mo khoa them khu dat.

## O dat va khu vuc

Vuon duoc chia thanh nhieu o dat. Mot so khu vuc co the bi khoa bang lop may/khung khoa.

- Khu dau tien mo san de ban bat dau trong cay.
- Khu moi duoc mo khi tai khoan dat yeu cau XP.
- O dat bi khoa khong the trong cay cho den khi khu do duoc mo.

## Trong hoa

1. Mo tui do.
2. Chon hat giong.
3. Cham vao o dat trong.
4. Neu thao tac hop le, cay se xuat hien tren o dat.

Meo: hay uu tien phu kin cac o dat dang mo khoa truoc khi mua nhieu decor.

## Cham soc cay

Cac vat pham cham soc giup cay tang XP va lon len:

| Vat pham | Cong dung |
| --- | --- |
| Binh tuoi | Tang XP cho cay va lam dat trong trang thai duoc tuoi |
| Phan bon | Tang XP nhieu hon binh tuoi |
| Thuoc | Ho tro cham soc va tang XP cho cay |

Cach dung:

1. Mo tui do.
2. Chon vat pham cham soc.
3. Cham hoac keo qua cay/o dat muon cham soc.
4. Neu vat pham du so luong va cay hop le, game se ap dung hanh dong.

## Tien hoa va thu hoach

Cay co nhieu giai doan phat trien. Khi nhan du XP, cay se len giai doan cao hon.

- Cay non: moi trong, can cham soc.
- Cay dang lon: da co tien do XP.
- Cay truong thanh: co the thu hoach neu dat dieu kien.

De thu hoach:

1. Bat che do thu hoach bang nut hinh luoi liem.
2. Cham vao cay da truong thanh.
3. San pham thu hoach se duoc dua vao tui do.
4. Tat che do thu hoach khi khong dung nua.

## Dao cay

Nut xeng dung de bat/tat che do dao cay. Khi che do nay dang bat, cham vao cay/o dat de thao tac theo che do dao.

Chi nen dung khi ban chac chan muon don o dat, vi viec dao co the lam mat cay hien tai tuy cau hinh game.

## Synergy giua cac loai hoa

Mot so loai hoa khi duoc dat gan nhau trong cung khu co the tao hieu ung cong huong.

- Neu khu co synergy, game se hien chi bao quanh vung do.
- Synergy thuong duoc dung de khuyen khich trong da dang loai hoa.
- Neu khong thay synergy, thu thay doi bo cuc hoa trong cung khu.

## Decor trong vuon

Decor giup trang tri vuon.

1. Mo tui do.
2. Chon tab decor.
3. Chon mon decor.
4. Cham vao vi tri trong vuon de dat.

Neu muon thu hoi decor, cham vao decor da dat va dung nut thu hoi neu nut nay xuat hien.
